    intervention_grammar.knowledge_operators ¤

    Knowledge operators change what is known about a structure.

    Record and Probe leave the structure as it is and have no inverse: what has been learnt stays known. OpenUp also changes the building, since a covering comes off, so it has an inverse, Close.

    Classes:

    • Record –

      Record something about a member, or about the structure.

    • Probe –

      Inspect a member at a station.

    • OpenUp –

      Remove a covering to expose a member and its connections.

    • Close –

      Cover an opened member again.

    Probe ¤

    Probe(member, at=None, finding=None)

    Bases: Operation

    Inspect a member at a station.

    It has no inverse.

    A finding of "sound" or "damaged" sets the member's condition.

    Parameters:

    • member (str) –

      The member to inspect.

    • at (float, default: None ) –

      Station of the inspection, if it is local.

    • finding (str, default: None ) –

      "sound", "damaged", or any description.
