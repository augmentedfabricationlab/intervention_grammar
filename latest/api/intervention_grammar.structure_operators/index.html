    intervention_grammar.structure_operators ¤

    Structure operators change what the finished structure is: its members, their connections, their lengths and their positions.

    Collapse and Bypass, which combine several structure operators, are in intervention_grammar.approaches.

    Classes:

    • Cut –

      Cut material out of a member.

    • Inlay –

      Fit a new piece into a member.

    • Split –

      Cut a member through at a station; the two pieces meet in a joint.

    • Merge –

      Make two connected pieces one member again.

    • Join –

      Join two members with a joint.

    • Unjoin –

      Take away the connection between two members.

    • Trim –

      Shorten a member at one end.

    • Extend –

      Lengthen a member at one end.

    • Turn –

      Turn a member that is out: end for end, or about its own axis.

    • Add –

      Add a member to the structure.

    • Remove –

      Take a member out of the structure for good; its connections go with it.

    • Double –

      Add a piece alongside a member, sharing its load path.

    • Support –

      Give a member a permanent support from the ground.

    • Reroute –

      Connect a member to another one instead.

    • SplitNode –

      Let some connections of a crowded node meet in a node of their own.

    • MergeNodes –

      Let the connections of two nodes meet in one.

    • Move –

      Move members permanently.

    • Swap –

      Two members that are out exchange their places in the structure.

    Split ¤

    Split(member, at, joint=None, pieces=None, start=None)

    Bases: Operation

    Cut a member through at a station; the two pieces meet in a joint.

    Inverse: Merge.

    The connections of the member go to the piece on their side of the cut, found from the station of each connection on the member (the connection attribute "at"). Where stations are not known, start lists the neighbours of the start piece; when it is given, it decides for all of them.

    Parameters:

    • member (str) –

      The member to cut through.

    • at (float) –

      Station of the cut, in metres from the start of the member.

    • joint (dict, default: None ) –

      The joint the two pieces meet in, for example {"family": "lap", "length": 0.3}.

    • pieces (list[str], default: None ) –

      Names of the start and end piece. Default member.a and member.b. One of them may be the member's own name.

    • start (list[str], default: None ) –

      Neighbours that go to the start piece. All others go to the end piece.

    Turn ¤

    Turn(member, mode='end', quarter=0)

    Bases: Operation

    Turn a member that is out: end for end, or about its own axis.

    Inverse: Turn in the opposite direction.

    Turning end for end reverses the member, so the stations of its connections are measured from the other end. A quarter turn about the axis changes which face points where.

    Parameters:

    • member (str) –

      The member to turn. It has to be out.

    • mode (str, default: 'end' ) –

      "end" (end for end) or "axis" (about the long axis).

    • quarter (int, default: 0 ) –

      Number of quarter turns about the axis, for mode "axis".
