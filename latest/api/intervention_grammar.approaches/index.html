    intervention_grammar.approaches ¤

    Approaches are interventions written as compositions of operators.

    Each approach looks at the model as it is when it starts and returns the operations in the order they are carried out, structure and process operators mixed. Running a program expands the approaches, so the log shows every single step and the approach it belongs to.

    Where an approach needs to know which connections lie in a zone of a member, it reads the stations of the connections (the connection attribute "at"), or takes them from its zone parameter.

    Classes:

    • Patch –

      Cut damaged material out of part of a member and fit a new piece.

    • Prosthesis –

      Replace a member over its full section along a span.

    • Replacement –

      Replace a whole member.

    • Reinforcement –

      Add a piece alongside a member and fasten the two together; the piece shares the member's load path.

    • Addition –

      Introduce a new member that takes part of the load; the damage stays.

    • Reconnection –

      Remake a failed connection with a separate connector.

    • Realignment –

      Move members back into line and secure them again.

    • Subtraction –

      Take damaged or harmful material away without replacing it.

    • Repositioning –

      Take a member out, turn it, and put it back, so that its damaged parts lie where they matter less.

    • Collapse –

      Remove a bay and close the structure up (an edge ring collapse).

    • Bypass –

      Remove a member and let a neighbour that ended on it continue to the next member.

    • Lowering –

      Shorten the members standing on a base, replace the base and lower everything above onto it.

    • Retain –

      Leave the structure as it is and record why.

    Prosthesis ¤

    Prosthesis(
    member, span, joint=None, zone=None, path="straight"
)

    Bases: Approach

    Replace a member over its full section along a span.

    Where the span reaches an end of the member, the new piece is joined to the rest of the member at one cut; where it lies in the middle, at two cuts.

    Prosthesis = Release(connections in the span) ; Split ; Remove(old piece) ; Add(new piece) ; Insert ; Seat ; Join(rest of the member) ; Join(connections in the span) ; Secure. In the middle, the member is split twice and the new piece is joined to both parts.

    The rest of the member keeps its name; in the middle, the part after the span becomes member.b. The old piece becomes member.old, the new one member.new. Where the old piece rested on a neighbour, the member now rests on the new piece.

    Parameters:

    • member (str) –

      The member to repair.

    • span (list[float]) –

      Stations of the start and the end of the replaced piece, in metres from the start of the member.

    • joint (dict | list[dict], default: None ) –

      The joint at the cuts, or one joint per cut: [at the start of the span, at its end].

    • zone (list[str], default: None ) –

      Neighbours connected within the span, where stations are not known.

    • path (str, default: 'straight' ) –

      How the new piece is brought in, see Insert. Default "straight".

    Shorten the members standing on a base, replace the base and lower everything above onto it.

    The topology stays the same.
