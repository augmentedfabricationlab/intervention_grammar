    Operators¤

    Every operator is a class with check, apply and inverse. The operators fall into four layers. Structure operators change what the finished structure is. Joint operators change the shape of a connection. Process operators move the structure through temporary states on the way. Knowledge operators change what is known about it.

    Members and connections are named by the names of their elements: a connection is given by its two members a and b. Positions along a member are stations, in metres from its start.

    Structure¤

    What the finished structure is: its members, their connections, their lengths and their positions.

    Operator Parameters What it does Inverse
    Cut member, region Cut material out of a member. Inlay
    Inlay member, piece, region, joint Fit a new piece into a member. Remove of the piece
    Split member, at, joint, pieces, start Cut a member through at a station; the two pieces meet in a joint. Merge
    Join a, b, joint, state, at, support Join two members with a joint. Unjoin
    Unjoin a, b Take away the connection between two members. Join
    Merge a, b, into Make two connected pieces one member again. Split
    Trim member, end, by Shorten a member at one end. Extend
    Extend member, end, by Lengthen a member at one end. Trim
    Turn member, mode, quarter Turn a member that is out: end for end, or about its own axis. Turn in the opposite direction
    Add member, like, element, placed, connections, attributes Add a member to the structure. Remove
    Remove member Take a member out of the structure for good; its connections go with it. Add
    Double member, piece, face, span, joint Add a piece alongside a member, sharing its load path. Remove of the piece
    Support member, at, support, kind Give a member a permanent support from the ground. Remove of the support
    Reroute member, old, new, at, state Connect a member to another one instead. Reroute to the old member
    SplitNode node, connections, new Let some connections of a crowded node meet in a node of their own. MergeNodes
    MergeNodes node, other Let the connections of two nodes meet in one. SplitNode
    Move members, by Move members permanently. Move by the opposite vector
    Swap a, b Two members that are out exchange their places in the structure. Swap

    Remove is undone geometrically: the member comes back in its shape, in new material. The same holds for Cut, which an inlay undoes, and for Fill. The share of original material that remains is measured with original_share.

    Joint¤

    The shape of a connection. Joints are plain dicts, a family name and its parameters, for example {"family": "lap", "length": 0.3}. The grammar stores and changes them and leaves the meaning of a family to the tools that make or check the joint, so joints can come from any source.

    Operator Parameters What it does Inverse
    Select a, b, joint Set the joint of a connection. Select with the previous joint
    Orient a, b, turns Turn the joint about the member axis by quarter turns. Orient by the opposite number of turns
    Tilt a, b, angle, plane Tilt a cutting plane of the joint by an angle. Tilt by the opposite angle
    Lengthen a, b, by Make the joint longer. Shorten
    Shorten a, b, by Make the joint shorter. Lengthen
    Mirror a, b Mirror the joint. Mirror
    AddFeature a, b, feature Add a feature to the joint. Select with the joint as it was
    Fasten a, b, fastener, count Add fasteners to the joint. Select with the joint as it was
    Detach a, b Make the connecting part of a joint a separate piece. Select with the joint as it was
    Open a, b, towards Open a joint on one side, so that a member can be inserted from that side. Select with the joint as it was
    Ease a, b, play Give the joint a clearance for assembly. Select with the joint as it was

    Process¤

    The temporary states of an intervention: fastenings undone and made again, members taken out and put back, the structure lifted and propped.

    Operator Parameters What it does Inverse
    Release a, b Release a connection: undo its fastening, the members stay in contact. Secure
    Secure a, b Fasten a connection. Release
    Withdraw member, path Take a member out without destroying it, for reuse or turning. Insert
    Insert member, path Bring a member that is out into place along a path. Withdraw
    Seat member Press an inserted member fully home. Unseat
    Unseat member Loosen a seated member so it can be withdrawn. Seat
    Lift members, by Jack members up. Lower
    Lower members, by Lower jacked members back. Lift
    Prop member, at Put a temporary prop under a member. Unprop
    Unprop member, at Take a temporary prop away. Prop
    Machine member, end, joint Cut a joint into the end of a member. Fill
    Fill member, end Fill a machined end with new material: the shape comes back, the original material does not. Machine

    Knowledge¤

    What is known about the structure. Record and Probe add knowledge and have no inverse.

    Operator Parameters What it does Inverse
    Record text, member Record something about a member, or about the structure. none
    Probe member, at, finding Inspect a member at a station. none
    OpenUp member, what Remove a covering to expose a member and its connections. Close
    Close member, what Cover an opened member again. OpenUp
