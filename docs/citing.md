# Citing

Intervention Grammar is developed at the
[Professorship of Digital Fabrication](https://www.arc.ed.tum.de/df),
Technical University of Munich.

If you use it for your work, please cite it with:

```latex
@misc{intervention_grammar,
    title={{Intervention Grammar}: operators and approaches for interventions in existing structures},
    author={Rein, Tizian},
    howpublished={https://github.com/augmentedfabricationlab/intervention_grammar},
    note={Professorship of Digital Fabrication, Technical University of Munich},
    year={2026},
}
```
